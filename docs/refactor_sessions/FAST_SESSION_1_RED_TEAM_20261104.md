# Fast completion session 1 — independent review

The author reported full offline completion and full-suite success on a working tree based on `9f575b0`. Those test claims were not independently reproduced on the final tree in this review and do not establish the broader architectural acceptance criteria. Existing S4–S6 verdicts remain partial.

Findings from inspection:

- `OperationalBudgets.admit_token_reservation` and `admit_kaggle_job` exist, but searches for their production callers find only their definitions: the actual autonomous loop still calls `has_tokens_available`, and the separate Kaggle broker remains. Python counter synchronization is not transactional admission and does not make the budget system unified. Provider gateway accounting is a separate path. Do not launch a paid campaign assuming the requested operational ceilings are globally enforced.
- The OLS improvement in `s6_gates.rs` trains weights, a meaningful correction to the previous hardcoded prediction fixture. It does not by itself prove that a real owner uses research evidence to choose, train, compare and challenge hypotheses. Real live research and Kaggle telemetry are unverified.
- Repair task errors now propagate and a repair branch is used; however `repair_base_hash` remains a formatted project/epoch string rather than a content digest. Complete code, data, environment and release provenance and atomic fencing remain open.
- The newly added legacy DAG migration suppressed errors, dropped the temporary table and performed a multi-statement rebuild outside a transaction. This review replaces it with a serialized, rechecked, transactional rebuild with foreign-key verification and error propagation.
- Resource accounting admitted negative monetary values and negative or nonfinite Kaggle settlement, released uncertain Kaggle reservations, allowed an unknown token settlement to reduce its reservation, and silently accepted conflicting evidence on repeat search settlement. This review validates inputs, retains uncertain reservations, rejects reduced unknown token liabilities and checks evidence identity. Provider accounting queries now propagate errors rather than silently converting failures to zero. These local repairs do not wire the authority into real dispatch or reconcile remote usage.
- The status report previously treated absent or failed provider accounting queries as verified zeros. Errors now propagate, and missing provider accounting is marked unverified. Even a successfully queried local ledger does not prove global enforcement or remote telemetry.
- The new configured provider/model, route and pricing have not been independently verified against a live provider, nor has a paid or Amazon run been conducted during this review. Model availability, routing, pricing, policy and actual enforcement must be preflighted before dispatch.
